How a referral works

  1. Call us or send the form on this page. Tell us a little about the person's needs and funding. Please do not put names, birth dates or other private health details in the form.
  2. We call you back to talk through care needs, routines, mobility and timing, and whether Blossom Hill is a good fit.
  3. Send documents by fax to 877-912-0759 so we can review the referral.
  4. Tour the home. The person and their family or guardian are welcome to visit before deciding.
  5. Assessment and move-in. We complete our assessment and service plan and coordinate the move-in date with you.

What helps us review a referral

Please fax what you have. We can start the conversation without all of it.

  • Current support plan or MnCHOICES assessment summary
  • Diagnosis list and recent history and physical
  • Current medication list and physician orders
  • Notes on mobility, transfers, diet and any behavior support needs
  • Contact details for the guardian or responsible party, if there is one

Is Blossom Hill a good fit?

Every person is different. If you are not sure whether we can meet someone's needs, call us at 763-285-9666 and we will talk it through honestly. Our services, accessibility and FAQ pages have more detail, and our Uniform Disclosure of Assisted Living Services and Amenities (UDALSA) is available on request.
