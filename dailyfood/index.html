How to make it

Safe cooking temperatures (check with a food thermometer)
  • Chicken and turkey: 165°F
  • Ground beef: 160°F
  • Beef roasts: at least 145°F
  • Fish and shrimp: 145°F (fish patties made with egg: 155°F)
  • Eggs: cook until firm, no runny egg (160°F)
  • Reheating leftovers: 165°F
  • Keep hot food at 135°F or above
  • Keep cold food at 41°F or below

The ingredients and amounts are on the menu above and match the number of servings. The steps work for any number of servings (for example "1 patty per person").
