What Is Customized Living in Minnesota?

If you are looking at assisted living in Minnesota, you will often hear the words "customized living." Here is what it means in plain language.

Customized living, in short

In Minnesota, customized living is the name for assisted living services paid for by a waiver. The services are put together in an individual plan for each person, based on what they need help with. 24-hour customized living is the same thing with supervision around the clock.

Customized living can be paid for by the Brain Injury (BI) waiver, the CADI waiver and the Elderly Waiver (EW), and it is usually provided in assisted living settings.

Who can provide it

Under Minnesota law, a provider of customized living services generally must be licensed by the Minnesota Department of Health as an assisted living facility (Minnesota Statutes chapter 144G). You can ask any home for its license number.

What customized living can include

Every plan is different. Services often include help with:

  • Bathing, dressing, grooming and using the bathroom
  • Medications, set up and given on time
  • Meals and snacks that fit a person's diet
  • Moving around safely, including wheelchair users
  • Housekeeping and laundry
  • Supervision, including overnight, for 24-hour customized living

Questions to ask a home

  • Do you provide customized living or 24-hour customized living, and which waivers do you accept?
  • May I see your UDALSA? This is the Uniform Disclosure of Assisted Living Services and Amenities, a state form that lists what the home offers.
  • Who is awake and on site at night?
  • How is the service plan made and updated, and how is the family included?
  • May I see the contract and the Minnesota Assisted Living Bill of Rights?
