Frequently Asked Questions About Blossom Hill

For residents on the CADI or Brain Injury (BI) waiver, the county waiver pays for the care services. Private-pay rates depend on the care each person needs, so we give each family a clear monthly price after we talk about their needs. Call 763-285-9666, Monday to Friday, 9:00 am to 5:00 pm, for current rates.

Our team of compassionate caregivers is dedicated to providing personalized care to each resident. We focus on building strong relationships and understanding each individual’s needs. Our staff is highly trained and attentive, ensuring that your loved one receives the highest level of care. We also provide open communication with families so that you are always in the loop about your loved one’s well-being.

Caregivers are on site 24 hours a day, and a registered nurse (RN) oversees care plans and medication administration. We work closely with each resident’s doctors, pharmacy and family, and help residents get to medical appointments and other services they need. Blossom Hill is a licensed assisted living facility, not a nursing home, so please call us at 763-285-9666 to talk about specific health needs.

We offer a variety of engaging activities designed to stimulate the mind, body, and spirit. From arts and crafts to light exercise sessions, residents have the opportunity to participate in activities that foster social interaction and personal fulfillment. Whether your loved one enjoys quiet reflection or group participation, there’s something for everyone to enjoy.

Family and friends are always welcome to visit! We believe that maintaining strong connections with loved ones is essential for emotional well-being. Whether in person or through video calls, we make it easy for families to stay connected with their loved ones.

Safety is our top priority. Blossom Hill is equipped with security features like 24/7 supervision, emergency response systems, and secure entry points. Our staff is trained in safety protocols and emergency procedures to ensure the well-being of all residents. We also provide personalized care plans that address any specific health or mobility concerns.

Blossom Hill offers both private and shared living spaces, allowing families to choose the best option based on their loved one’s preferences. Our rooms are designed for comfort, safety, and privacy, ensuring that each resident feels at home.

Blossom Hill Assisted Living is at 6765 Buckingham Road, Woodbury, MN 55125, in Washington County. We are just off I-494, about a five-minute drive from M Health Fairview Woodwinds Hospital and a four-minute walk from Carver Lake Park. See our location page for a map.

Blossom Hill serves adults 55 and older who need daily support, such as help with dressing, bathing, meals, mobility and medications. Our home is wheelchair accessible and can accommodate bariatric residents.

Yes. Blossom Hill is a Medicaid waiver provider. We accept waiver services such as CADI and BI. We also accept private pay.

The cost depends on the room and on the care each resident needs. Call us at 763-285-9666 or send a message and we will go over the cost for your situation, including waiver and private pay options.

Yes. Blossom Hill has a wheelchair ramp installed by Lifeway Mobility, accessible bathrooms with roll-in showers, wide hallways and extra-wide doorways. We also offer bariatric accommodations. See our accessibility page for photos.

Our availability page shows whether we currently have openings. You can also call 763-285-9666 for the most up-to-date answer.

Families, case managers and social workers can call 763-285-9666 or use our contact page to send a message. Our office is open Monday to Friday, 9:00 am to 5:00 pm. You can also view our brochure.

Yes. Blossom Hill LLC is a licensed assisted living facility in Minnesota and follows Minnesota's assisted living law (Minnesota Statutes chapter 144G). Our Uniform Disclosure of Assisted Living Services and Amenities (UDALSA), which lists the services we offer, is available on request.

Residents receive three home-cooked meals a day plus snacks. Meals are balanced and can be adapted to each resident's dietary needs and preferences.
